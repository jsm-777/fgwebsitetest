## Recommendation

Use the commissioned photography as a cast, not as a conventional portrait gallery. The strongest Version 2 direction combines:

- dark editorial frames for the opening and high-contrast transitions;
- clean white-background action frames for the “I am…” pathways;
- gray-stage movement images for cinematic section breaks;
- tactile prop details for services, resources, and footer transitions;
- Jessica's full-body movement portraits for the founder story instead of returning to a headshot-led homepage.

The 17 gallery favorites are useful but too narrow to drive the site by themselves: they overrepresent the fitness sequence and include only two Jessica frames. The broader proof and GIF selections reveal a far more inclusive arts ecosystem and should form the primary visual system.

## Homepage composition

The opening should use no more than five visible figures at once:

1. `14244` as the dominant movement image.
2. `12999` as the dark editorial counterpoint.
3. `14385` as a small airborne accent.
4. `13553` as the music cue.
5. `14481` as the founder reveal after the opening resolves, not as the first focal point.

On mobile, reduce this to one dominant frame (`14244`) plus one secondary cue (`12999` or `13553`). Do not load the full desktop montage.
