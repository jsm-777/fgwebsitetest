## Revised direction

The Version 2 opening and audience system will be designed from the best images in `GIF Still Frames`. The gallery Favorites are not a selection source and will not determine the homepage.

The still-frame album contains eight intentionally matched subjects. This is stronger than a conventional portrait gallery because each subject has a short movement sequence. Version 2 can therefore feel choreographed while remaining fast and accessible:

- use one strongest still per subject as the default image;
- optionally advance to a second frame on hover or focus on desktop;
- show the complete static composition when reduced motion is requested;
- load only one or two subjects in the mobile opening;
- preserve the clean white field so the cast can move between Paper and Stage Black layouts.

## Homepage composition

The desktop opening should use five primary stills:

1. `13403 1` as the dominant fabric-and-motion anchor.
2. `13513` as the contrasting teal vertical.
3. `13592` as the airborne music cue.
4. `13901` as the film/production cue.
5. `14002` as the brand-pink creative-business cue.

These should enter in a restrained canon sequence and resolve into a stable composition. They should not continuously animate.

The “I am…” section then completes the cast with `13629`, `13849`, and `13943`. On hover or keyboard focus, each pathway may transition once to its secondary frame.

On mobile, use `13403 1` as the primary image and `14002` as the secondary cue. Do not download the entire cast before interaction.

## Treatment notes

- Prefer object-position and masks over aggressive crops.
- Do not remove the white background until treatment rights are confirmed; Stage Black compositions can place the full white image inside deliberate editorial frames.
- Keep faces, hands, fabric edges, hats, instruments, clapperboards, palettes, plans, and feet inside the crop-safe area.
- The youth-program images stay out of the hosted preview until model-release approval is confirmed.
- The founder and object-detail images from `Proofs` can support later pages, but they do not lead or define the homepage selection.
