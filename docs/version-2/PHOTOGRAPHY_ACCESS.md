Before selected photographs are committed or published, confirm:

- Financial Groove may use the selected images on its website and Vercel previews.
- Whether crops, background removal, monochrome treatment, color grading, or compositing are permitted.
- Required photographer credit wording and placement.
- Whether high-resolution downloads are available and permitted.
- Whether any pictured models, minors, brands, artwork, or venues create placement restrictions.
