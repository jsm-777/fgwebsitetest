## Shared Drive findings

The archive contains more than copy. Its repeated ideas are consistent across independent documents:

- The site should feel like an experience, not an accounting template.
- Movement is part of the brand story, especially a cascading or canon-like sequence across creative disciplines.
- “I Am…” should help visitors recognize themselves before choosing a service.
- The interface should be visually simple, with an unobtrusive menu and a controlled amount of scrolling.
- Photography should show action, gesture, fabric, instruments, tools, and behind-the-scenes detail—not generic business imagery.
- Financial Groove should feel technically rigorous, approachable, arts-literate, and distinctly unlike a traditional accounting firm.
- The brand tension is intentional: polished gallery clarity against industrial texture and imperfect edges.

The archive also includes ideas that should not be revived without current approval:

- Former team biographies and roles
- Named client/organization claims
- New York office language
- Membership, ecommerce, events, multiple client-portal providers, and a service quiz
- Old scheduling, social, and third-party platform references
- Old price, package, guarantee, deduction, and “maximize return” language

## Current live-site audit

Reviewed October 22, 2026 at `https://financialgroove.com/`.

### What is valuable

- The opening is immersive and art-led: full-screen monochrome dance imagery, sparse chrome, and a compact hamburger menu.
- The site immediately communicates “Accounting Expertise for the Arts & Entertainment.”
- “Bridging the Gap Between Creativity & Business” and “Financial Groove exists to advance the arts” remain strong brand foundations.
- The published Las Vegas contact information aligns with Version 1.
- Existing testimonials provide useful evidence for a future permissions review.

### What should change

- The opening visual atmosphere is stronger than the information architecture beneath it.
- The page remains incomplete and explicitly says “Work in Progress.”
- Audience pathways are inconsistent and several lack destinations.
- Service links and legacy content do not form a dependable conversion journey.
- Accessibility, responsive content hierarchy, performance, metadata, and structured navigation need the modern foundation already established in Version 1.
